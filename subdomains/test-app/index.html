The Product

Stair-Climbing Intelligence, Built for Cities

Adaptive Terrain Mode

Sensor fusion detects stairs, angle changes, and edge discontinuities, then dynamically adjusts torque and center of gravity in real time.

AI Stability Loop 2.0

Fore-aft stabilization is extended with predictive balancing, gyroscopic response, and load-adaptive control to remove tilt sensation during transitions.

Modular Ground Modules

Urban Wheel, Cluster Stair, and Rugged Mode modules enable fast pavement travel and confident operation on snow, cobblestone, and construction surfaces.
